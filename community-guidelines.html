Rallysh Community Guidelines

Effective date: September 1, 2026

Rallysh is for respectful tennis communities. Use the Report and Block tools whenever you encounter content or conduct that breaks these rules.

Be respectful

Do not harass, bully, threaten, shame, discriminate against, impersonate, or target another person. Do not publish private information without permission.

Post authentic tennis content

Only share photos you have the right to use and match results you believe are accurate. Do not post sexual, violent, hateful, illegal, deceptive, spam, or copyrighted material you are not permitted to share.

Moderation

Rallysh filters prohibited language, lets users report content and block accounts, and reviews reports. We may remove content, restrict features, or suspend accounts when content violates these Guidelines or our Terms. Repeated or serious violations can lead to permanent suspension.
