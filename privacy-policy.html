Rallysh helps recreational tennis players record matches, maintain rankings, and share match moments.

Information we collect

  • Profile information: name, email address, date of birth, gender, and optional profile photo.
  • Match information: participants, scores, match format, approval status, rankings, and timestamps.
  • Optional content: court photos, venue names, comments, and likes.
  • Optional location information: only when you choose to tag a court or venue.

How we use information

We use this information to provide player profiles, rankings, match approval, match sharing, venue tagging, support, and app security.

Sharing

Match details and posts may be visible to players in the relevant tennis group. We do not sell personal information. We share data with service providers only when needed to operate the app, such as hosting, authentication, storage, and notification providers.

Your choices

Security and retention

Children

Rallysh is not directed to children under 13. Do not use the app if you are under 13.
